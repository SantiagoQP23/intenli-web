## 1. What Intenli Money is

Intenli Money is an app to keep track of your money: accounts, transactions, debts to pay and to collect, and budget and savings boxes, in one or more spaces (personal, business or shared). It works on Android, iPhone and the web, and also offline.

It's a **record-keeping tool**. It isn't a bank, doesn't move real money, doesn't connect to your bank accounts, and doesn't give financial, accounting, tax or legal advice. The decisions you make based on what you see in the app are yours.

## 6. Amounts, currencies and totals

The app calculates balances and totals from what you record. If you use several currencies, totals are converted with the exchange rates you enter; if one is missing, the app says so instead of guessing. Check your records: we don't guarantee they match your bank statements or that they're valid as accounting or tax records.
